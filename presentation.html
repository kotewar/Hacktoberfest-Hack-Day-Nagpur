💡 The Solution

EduGemma: Unlimited Learning in Airplane Mode

A single unified desktop workstation turning any standard low-cost laptop into an elite 24/7 personal tutor.

⚡
100% On-Device & Zero Internet

🌟 Key Capabilities

The 4 Core Superpowers of EduGemma

1. 📖 Unified Knowledge Canvas
Reads pre-loaded syllabi (Biology, Physics, Chemistry, CS/AI) or authors chapters on-the-fly. Includes 1-click shortcuts: Explain Simply, Village/Home Analogy, and Formulas.
2. 🤖 Socratic Tutor with Visible Thinking
Refuses to spoon-feed answers. Guides with probing questions, reveals its Inner Pedagogical Thought Process, and shows live local token inference speed.
3. 🗺️ Visual Mind Maps & CS Traces
Synthesizes responsive Mermaid flowcharts and data structures (Self-Attention pipelines, Backpropagation, BSTs, Dynamic Programming).
4. 🎯 Active Recall & Spaced Repetition
Generates instant 3-question MCQs and evaluates subjective short answers out of 5 marks, automatically updating the student's mastery matrix.
